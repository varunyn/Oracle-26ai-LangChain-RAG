## Design Context

### Users
Individual users use this app to chat with an LLM in a RAG workflow. Their context is task-focused question answering: they open the app to ask questions, retrieve grounded answers from their document collection, review citations, and optionally manage uploaded knowledge sources.

### Brand Personality
Oracle-branded, trustworthy, enterprise, technical. The interface should feel reliable, clear, and professional rather than playful or experimental.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode is the primary target and is sufficient for current needs. The visual direction should align with Oracle enterprise product expectations: clean, structured, low-friction, and citation-oriented. It should not feel generic AI-demo-like, overly decorative, neon, glassy, or trend-chasing.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity over novelty: retrieval settings, model controls, citations, and upload state should be immediately understandable.
2. Reinforce trust: grounded answers, visible sources, and stable interaction patterns should feel dependable and enterprise-ready.
3. Keep the UI lightweight: avoid decorative motion and visual effects that distract from question answering.
4. Optimize for focused work: layouts should support long reading sessions, scanning citations, and managing RAG options without clutter.
5. Use Oracle-appropriate restraint: polished and professional, but not visually loud.
